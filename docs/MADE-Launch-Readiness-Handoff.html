Open Decisions Need Your Input

Three things that need a call from you before we lock the launch.

1. Booking experience

The Pabau booking widget is functional and secure, but it has its own look that doesn’t fully match the site. For May 1, the recommendation is to ship as-is — appointments work, payments work, calendars sync.

A custom booking flow (services list with imagery → tap to open Pabau on the right service) is the “barbershop-style” experience. It’s a real build, not a polish job. Best handled as a Phase 2 project after launch.

Decision: ship Pabau widget as-is for May 1?

2. Shop platform

Pabau doesn’t handle full e-commerce checkout the way we’d hoped. Right now the shop page is “inquire”-style — clients see the product and reach out. That works for May 1.

To accept card payments online for products, the realistic path is Shopify (or Shopify Lite at $9/mo) integrated with the existing site. That’s a separate build with its own scope and cost.

Decision: launch with inquire-style shop, revisit Shopify integration later?

3. Hero & section background images

On /services and /about, the background images load over the network from storage — you may notice a brief flash before they settle. That’s because they’re admin-swappable.

If you’re happy with the current backgrounds, we can “bake them in” to the site code. Trade-off: instant load, no flash, lower bandwidth bill — but you can’t swap them yourself anymore (we’d swap on request).

Decision: bake in the current backgrounds, or keep them admin-swappable?

Getting the Most Out of the Site

The site looks great when it’s fed great content. Here are the specs that keep everything looking sharp as you upload.

Image specs by section

Where Recommended size Format
Service photos 1600 × 1000 px JPG / WebP
Shop product photos 1200 × 1200 px (square) JPG / PNG, neutral background
Team headshots 900 × 1200 px (vertical) JPG, professional lighting
Testimonial avatars 400 × 400 px (square) JPG / PNG
Section backgrounds 2400 × 1600 px JPG, under 500 KB
Social share image (OG) 1200 × 630 px (exact) JPG / PNG

Content rules of thumb

  • Keep shop product descriptions under ~250 characters Longer descriptions stretch the cards out of proportion
  • Use consistent product photo backgrounds (all white, or all neutral) Mixed backgrounds make the shop look uneven
  • Compress photos before uploading — use TinyPNG or Squoosh Big files slow page load and chew bandwidth
  • Use real client first names on testimonials when you have permission “Sarah M.” outperforms “Verified Client”
  • Update Pabau as your source of truth, then sync to the site Avoids the data living in two places that disagree
Need a refresher later? The admin panel has tooltips on each upload field. We’ll also do a 60-minute training session post-launch where you walk through every admin page while I’m on call.
