Custom-Built • Not a Template

What Was Built

Your website is a fully custom-coded website — not built on a template platform like Wix or Squarespace. Every page, animation, and feature was designed and written specifically for MADE Med Spa.

Think of it this way: Wix and Squarespace are like renting an apartment — you can decorate, but you can't knock down walls. Your site is like owning the building. You control everything, and nothing is limited by someone else's rules.

The site is built with technologies used by companies like Netflix, Nike, TikTok, and Hulu. These are industry-standard, professional-grade tools — not experimental or niche.

Your Pages

  • Home Hero video, featured services, testimonials, newsletter
  • About Your story, your team, your approach
  • Services Full catalog with individual detail pages for each service
  • Membership Membership tiers with benefits and pricing
  • Shop Product catalog for skincare and retail

The Technology Behind It

You don't need to understand the technical details to run your business — that's what your developer is for. But here's a friendly overview of what powers your site, in case you're ever asked.

◈

Next.js

The framework that builds your website. Used by Nike, TikTok, Hulu, and thousands of businesses. It makes the site extremely fast and great for search engines (Google).

◈

React

The technology that makes the site feel smooth and interactive. Buttons, menus, animations — all powered by React, created by Meta (Facebook).

◈

Convex

Your database and content management system. This is where your services, team bios, FAQs, testimonials, products, and memberships are stored. You manage it through your admin panel.

◈

Clerk

Handles secure login for your admin panel. Only authorized people can access the back end of the site to make changes.

◈

Vercel

Where your site lives on the internet (hosting). Vercel is made by the same company that builds Next.js — so they work perfectly together. Fast, secure, and reliable.

◈

Framer Motion

The animation engine. Every smooth fade, slide, and hover effect on the site is powered by this. It gives the site a premium, editorial feel.

Your Admin Panel

You have a private admin area at yoursite.com/admin where you can manage your website content without needing a developer for everyday updates.

Services

Add, edit, reorder, or hide services. Each one gets its own detail page automatically.

Team

Update team member bios, photos, and titles.

FAQs

Add or edit frequently asked questions, organized by category.

Testimonials

Manage client testimonials shown on the site.

Contact Leads

View form submissions from potential clients.

Site Content

Update headlines, text, and images across the site.

Memberships

Create and manage membership tiers, pricing, and benefits.

Shop Products

Add retail products with descriptions, pricing, and images.

No coding required. The admin panel is a simple, visual interface. If you can use Instagram, you can update your website.

Custom-Built vs. Template Platforms

Here's how your custom site compares to common website platforms:

Feature Your Site Wix / Squarespace
Design ✓ 100% custom to your brand Based on shared templates
Speed ✓ Optimized for fast loading Can be slow due to bloat
Google / SEO ✓ Built-in best practices Limited control
Monthly platform fee ✓ None (just hosting) $16–$49/month
Animations ✓ Premium, smooth, custom Basic presets only
Ownership ✓ You own the code Locked to their platform
Scalability ✓ Unlimited growth Limited by plan tier
Admin panel ✓ Custom-built for you Generic, one-size-fits-all
You own everything. If you ever switch developers, the entire codebase belongs to you. With Wix or Squarespace, if you leave, you start from scratch.

Security & Performance

Your website was built with security and speed as priorities, not afterthoughts.

Security

Protected Admin Access

Only authorized users can log into the admin panel. Authentication is handled by Clerk, an industry-leading security provider.

Security Headers

The site includes protections against common web attacks (clickjacking, XSS, content sniffing) — all configured automatically.

Performance

Fast Loading

Images and videos are optimized and cached so pages load quickly, even on slower connections.

Mobile-Friendly

The entire site is responsive — it looks great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Integrations

Your website is designed to work alongside the tools you already use to run your business.

Pabau (EMR System)

Your practice management software. The site links to Pabau for online booking, membership sign-ups, and product purchases — keeping everything in one system.

Newsletter

Built-in email capture. Visitors can subscribe directly from your site, and you can view subscribers in your admin panel.
